The quick answer

A SuperBuy spreadsheet is a way to organize product research. Keep the item name, source, chosen variant, measurements and unanswered questions together. Treat each entry as a lead to investigate, rather than a guarantee about the product.

A simple research routine

How to use a SuperBuy spreadsheet.

01 / DISCOVER

Choose one useful starting point.

A SuperBuy spreadsheet for beginners works best with a clear brief: one category, an intended use and the fit you want. Start with everyday shoes, a layer for cooler weather or one missing accessory.

02 / COMPARE

Give every lead the same fields.

Learning how to organize SuperBuy finds means recording comparable details. Note the source, color, size, material description and measurement units. Leave unknown information marked as unknown.

03 / VERIFY

Resolve the important gaps.

Use a SuperBuy product comparison checklist to identify what needs confirmation. Reopen the current listing, check the selected variant and ask about missing measurements before treating a saved entry as ready to order.

The reading room

Good questions make better finds.

Practical fashion spreadsheet buying notes for fit, visual inspection and a more complete order plan.

Article 01 / Fit & materials

Build a clothing shortlist around measurements.

A useful SuperBuy clothing spreadsheet puts dimensions beside each saved item. Begin with a garment you already wear comfortably. Measure it flat, using the same points shown in the listing. A SuperBuy clothing size chart is only helpful when you know whether it describes the body or the finished garment.

For T-shirt fabric weight comparison, separate any stated fabric weight from the total item weight. Neither figure alone tells you whether a shirt will feel soft, hold its shape or suit a warm climate. When learning how to compare material descriptions, prioritize composition, construction and care information over vague descriptions such as “premium.”

Use a jacket layering size guide based on what you plan to wear underneath. Compare the chest, shoulders and sleeves with a jacket that already accommodates those layers. For a SuperBuy shoes sizing guide, look for the specific model’s size information and measurement method; numbers from another shoe may not transfer.

Article 02 / The SuperBuy QC guide

Read QC photos as evidence, not a promise.

Learning how to read warehouse QC photos starts with separating what is visible from what is assumed. Compare the item’s overall shape, selected color, labels and visible condition. Lighting, camera angle and image processing can change how a color appears.

A sneaker QC photo checklist can cover the pair’s apparent symmetry, visible sole edges, laces, size labels and surface marks. A clothing stitching inspection guide should focus on accessible seams, hems, cuffs and fastenings. Ask for a closer view when a detail matters but cannot be seen clearly.

When comparing seller photos versus warehouse photos, remember that the former may illustrate a style while the latter may show a particular received item. Complete product variant verification by matching the order’s color and size with any visible labels. Neither type of image establishes authenticity.

Keep one “unconfirmed” field. Photos cannot reliably prove fiber composition, internal construction, comfort or long-term durability. Record those limits instead of filling gaps with guesses.

Article 03 / From shortlist to order

Turn product leads into a realistic order plan.

A SuperBuy shoes spreadsheet can help compare potential purchases, but saved entries become less useful when their links or options change. To understand how to check a product link, reopen it and confirm the item, selected variant, available information and current seller details. Record when you checked it.

Keep an international order budget checklist beside the product shortlist. Separate the item amount from any applicable domestic delivery, service charges, currency conversion, international freight and destination charges. A product total and a delivered total answer different questions.

A parcel weight and dimensions guide should account for both the packed weight and the package size. Ask which information an estimate uses and whether it can change after packing. Compare current options for your destination instead of assuming that another person’s parcel will cost the same.

The most useful shopping shortlist for beginners is short enough to review. Keep the strongest candidates, write down unresolved questions and remove entries that no longer fit your needs. Good SuperBuy finds are easier to assess when each has a clear reason to be on the list.

Straight answers

Before you save the next find.

A few clear answers about the guide, sizing, images and the limits of product research.

What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

A SuperBuy spreadsheet is a product-research list used to organize shopping leads, such as item names, source links, sizes and notes. A spreadsheet entry is a starting point for comparison, not proof of stock, authenticity or quality.

Is this an official SuperBuy website or a live spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent editorial guide, not an official SuperBuy service or a live inventory database. Category links open Cafans, and other browsing links lead to the Cafans homepage.

How do I choose the right clothing size?

Measure a similar garment that fits you, then compare the same measurement points with the specific listing. Check whether dimensions are flat garment measurements or body measurements, and confirm units before comparing.

Can QC photos prove a branded item is authentic?

No. QC photos can help reveal visible condition, labeling, color and some measurements. They do not establish authenticity, material composition, long-term durability or seller authorization.

Does an item price include the full order cost?

Do not assume so. Check which charges apply, including domestic delivery, service charges, currency conversion, international shipping and destination import charges. Use current checkout information rather than an old spreadsheet figure.

Are the reference images current Cafans listings?

No. The Nike, Gucci and Dior images illustrate style and inspection topics. They do not confirm that the pictured products are listed, available or authenticated on Cafans.
